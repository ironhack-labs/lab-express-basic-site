  • Supermodel (You Better Work)

    Becoming internationally famous

    RuPaul splashed into pop culture consciousness with his 1992 song “Supermodel (You Better Work),” which hit No. 45 on the Hot 100 and No. 2 on the Dance Club Songs chart

  • Rupaul's Drag Race

    Biggest Drag Competition

    The show documents RuPaul in the search for "America's next drag superstar". RuPaul plays the role of host, mentor, and head judge for this series, as contestants are given different challenges each week.

  • Emmy Award Winner

    12 winnings

    In winning the award, RuPaul extends his reign as the most-decorated Black artist in Emmys history, with 12 trophies to his name so far. He also won his first Tony Award in June.
