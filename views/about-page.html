About: Amalie Auguste Melitta Bentz

Early Life

Melitta Bentz was born in Dresden, Germany, on January 31, 1873, into a family of successful businessmen and entrepreneurs. Bentz's father, Karl Liebscher, was a publisher and books salesman, while her grandparents founded and owned a brewery. She married Johannes Emil Hugo Bentz, a small business owner ca.1899, and had three children, including two sons: Willy in 1899 and Horst in 1904; and one daughter by the name of Herta in 1911.
