70s Caê

A page dedicated to the work and life of Brazilian singer Caetano Veloso.


"In December 1968 Veloso was arrested - with Gilberto Gil - in São Paulo. They had their heads shaved, spent two months in prison and a further four months under house arrest. "The military wanted us to leave the country," says Veloso. "They let us play a concert to raise money for a plane ticket." As the rest of the world watched the moon landings on 21 July 1969, Veloso and Gil were preparing to leave Brazil. They wouldn't return for another three years..." (The Guardian)
