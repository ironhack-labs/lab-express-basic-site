Kobe Bryant was an American professional basketall player. A shhoting guard, he spent his 20-year entire career with the Los Angeles Lakers in the NBA. Widely regarder as one of the greatest basketball players of all time. Bryant won five NBA championships, was an 18-time All-Star, a 15-time member of the All-NBA Team, a 12-time member of the All-Defensive Team, the 2008 NBA Most Valuable Player (MVP), and a two-time NBA Finals MVP.
