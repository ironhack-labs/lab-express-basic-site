About

Donald McKinley Glover Jr., also known by his stage name Childish Gambino, was born September 25, 1983, at Edwards Air Force Base in Edwards, California, but was raised in Atlanta, Georgia.

In 2006, he graduated from the New York University Tisch School of the Arts with a degree in Dramatic Writing. That same year he was hired as a writer for the NBC sitcom 30 Rock.

He later rose to fame for portraying college student Troy Barnes on the NBC sitcom Community from 2009 to 2014.

Since 2016, Glover has starred in the TV series Atlanta, which he created and occasionally directs. For his work on Atlanta, Glover won various accolades, including two Primetime Emmy Awards and two Golden Globe Awards.

He has also starred in several successful films including the supernatural horror The Lazarus Effect (2015), the comedy-drama Magic Mike XXL (2015), and science fiction film The Martian (2015). He played Aaron Davis in the superhero film Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017), and played Lando Calrissian in the space western Solo: A Star Wars Story (2018). He also provided the voice of adult Simba in The Lion King (2019). As a filmmaker, he starred in and produced the short film Guava Island (2019).

Following independently released albums and mixtapes, he signed with Glassnote Records in 2011, and released his first studio album, Camp, that year. In 2013, he followed this with his second album, Because the Internet. Glover's third album, "Awaken, My Love!" (2016), spawned the single "Redbone", which peaked at number twelve on the Billboard Hot 100 and eventually earned him his first Grammy Award. In 2018, Glover released "This Is America", which debuted at number-one on the Hot 100, and won four Grammy Awards, including for Song and Record of the Year. Glover's fourth album, 3.15.20, was released in 2020.

He is known as a private person and rarely posts on social media or does interviews unless for promotional work. For that reason, not much about his personal life have been disclosed.

He has three sons with his partner, Michelle White.
