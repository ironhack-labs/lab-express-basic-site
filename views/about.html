Kurt Gödel: A Short Biography

Early Life

Kurt Gödel was born in Brünn (now Brno, Czech Republic) on April 28, 1906. He was the second of three children born to Rudolf Gödel, a lawyer, and his wife, Maria. Gödel's father was a member of the Austrian parliament and a prominent member of the Austrian Jewish community. Gödel's mother was a devout Catholic. Gödel's childhood was spent in Brünn, where he attended the local gymnasium. He was a brilliant student, and at the age of 16 he entered the University of Vienna to study mathematics. Gödel's studies were interrupted by World War I, and he served as a lieutenant in the Austrian army. After the war, Gödel returned to the University of Vienna and earned his doctorate in 1929.

Incompleteness Theorems

Mathematicians have long sought to reduce mathematics to a finite set of axioms and rules of inference. In 1931, Kurt Gödel demonstrated that this is impossible. Gödel's incompleteness theorems show that any consistent axiomatic system of mathematics, no matter how complete, must contain statements that are either true or false but cannot be proved or disproved within the system. Gödel's results have had a profound impact on the philosophy of mathematics and the foundations of mathematics.

Later Years

In 1933, Gödel left Austria for the United States, where he taught at Princeton University. He remained in the United States until his death in 1978. Gödel was a member of the National Academy of Sciences and the American Academy of Arts and Sciences. He was awarded the National Medal of Science in 1974.

Death

Later in his life, Gödel suffered periods of mental instability and illness. Following the assassination of his close friend Moritz Schlick, Gödel developed an obsessive fear of being poisoned, and would eat only food prepared by his wife Adele. Adele was hospitalized beginning in late 1977, and in her absence Gödel refused to eat; he weighed 29 kilograms when he died of "malnutrition and inanition caused by personality disturbance" in Princeton Hospital on January 14, 1978. He was buried in Princeton Cemetery. Adele died in 1981.
