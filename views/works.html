Our Photographers

Annie Leibovitz:
A portrait photographer known for her iconic images of celebrities, politicians, and cultural figures, Annie Leibovitz has become one of the most influential photographers of our time. Her style combines bold lighting and creative compositions to create powerful and often unconventional portraits.

Steve McCurry:
Renowned for his reportage photography, Steve McCurry has traveled the world capturing moments of human life in cultures across the globe. His iconic image of an Afghan girl has become one of the most recognizable photographs in the world.

Dorothea Lange:
A documentary photographer and photojournalist, Dorothea Lange's images of the Great Depression and Dust Bowl era have become iconic. Her work is known for its stark simplicity and ability to convey powerful emotions and social commentary through her lens.

Martin Parr:
A contemporary photographer known for his vibrant, often humorous depictions of modern life, Martin Parr has a unique vision and style that sets him apart from other photographers. He captures the absurdity and contradictions of our world in a way that is both humorous and insightful.
