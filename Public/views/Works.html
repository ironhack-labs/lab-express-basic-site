How Bunnie Lives?

The female reproductive tract is bipartite, which prevents an embryo from translocating between the uteri. The two uterine horns communicate with two cervix and form a vaginal canal. In addition to being bipartite, the female rabbit does not undergo an estrous cycle, which causes mating-induced ovulation.

The rabbit gestation period is short and ranges from 28 to 36 days with an average period of 31 days. A longer gestation period will generally yield a smaller litter while shorter gestation periods will give birth to a larger litter. The size of a single litter can range from four to 12 kits allowing a female to deliver up to 60 new kits a year. After birth, the female can become pregnant again as early as the next day

Breed, genetics, diet, gender, living conditions, and consistent healthcare all play a role in determining lifespan, but on average, most rabbits live 8 to 10 years. That being said, through good nutrition and quality wellness care, many rabbits are living to see 11, 12, or even older.
